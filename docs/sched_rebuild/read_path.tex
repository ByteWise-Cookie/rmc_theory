\begin{center}
{\LARGE\textbf{MC read data-return path}}\\[2pt]
{\small One channel, DDR5. DFI read capture $\rightarrow$ accumulate $\rightarrow$ RD\_SRAM $\rightarrow$
completion. All ports + timing explained. Widths parametric (DQ, N\_RANKS, 512=line). Mirror of the
write launch, but \textbf{PHY-timed}, not self-timed.}
\end{center}

\section{Shape of a read (vs a write)}
A RD command is issued on the CA bus; the data comes back \textbf{RL = CL} cycles later at the DRAM
pins. The MC does \emph{not} generate the data timing --- the \textbf{PHY drives} \texttt{dfi\_rddata}
with a \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_valid} strobe when the beats are actually present. The MC's job is:
\begin{itemize}
\item at RD issue, \textbf{arm} the capture: assert \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_en} at \texttt{cmd + tphy\_rdlat}
      (opens the capture window for \texttt{BL/2} beats), and push \{\texttt{dbuf\_addr, rob\_index}\}
      into a fixed-depth \textbf{RL delay line} so the destination is known when data lands;
\item \textbf{capture} beats on \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_valid}, accumulate into a 512b line, write \emph{once}
      to RD\_SRAM at \texttt{dbuf\_addr};
\item on the last beat, raise \texttt{rd\_done}; the ROB marks its bitmap; when all packets of a request
      are in, \texttt{MERGE} drains RD\_SRAM to the AXI R channel in order.
\end{itemize}
Key contrast with the write path: write data is \textbf{self-timed} (MC drives it out); read data is
\textbf{PHY-timed} (MC waits for \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_valid}). There is no ``en leads data'' offset the
MC owns --- the MC opens a window (\texttt{rddata\_en}) and the PHY fills it.

\section{Timing}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{tphy\_rdlat} --- delay (DFI cycles) from the RD command to when the MC asserts
      \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_en} (open the window). PHY-reported, DVFS-reloadable, \textbf{same across
      ranks} (per-rank skew absorbed in the PHY, invisible on DFI).
\item \textbf{RL = CL} --- command-to-data at the DRAM pins. The PHY maps the MC's window to the pin
      timing; the MC counts only DFI cycles.
\item \textbf{Capture window} --- \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_en} held for \texttt{BL/2} DFI-beats; PHY returns
      \texttt{dfi\_rddata} + \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_valid} within it. The MC keys capture off
      \texttt{rddata\_valid} (robust to small PHY skew), not a fixed offset.
\item \textbf{RL delay line} --- deterministic depth; loaded with \{\texttt{dbuf\_addr, rob\_index}\} at
      RD issue, pops at the capture point so the accumulator knows where to write. No CAM, no timestamp.
\item \textbf{dqFree} --- read bursts occupy the DQ bus \texttt{BL/2} = 8 CK each, same as writes;
      spacing / cross-rank tRTRS rules are identical to the write side (scoreboard).
\end{itemize}

\section{Gear / beat placement}
BL16 = 16 UI = 8 CK of data; a 64B line = 8 beat-groups of 8B (2 UI $\times$ DQ). Per mc\_clk the MC
captures \texttt{2*gear} beats across the active phases (mirror of write split):
\rowcolors{2}{white}{zeb}
\begin{tabular}{>{\footnotesize}l W W M}
\toprule
\rowcolor{hb}\textbf{gear} & \textbf{phases} & \textbf{mc\_clks} & \textbf{byte capture per mc\_clk}\\ \midrule
1:1 & p0        & 8 & p0: B0-7, then B8-15, \dots B56-63 (one beat-group/cyc, 8 cyc)\\
1:2 & p0,p1     & 4 & cyc0 p0=B0-7 p1=B8-15 ; cyc1 p0=B16-23 p1=B24-31 ; \dots\\
1:4 & p0..p3    & 2 & cyc0 p0..p3=B0-31 ; cyc1 p0..p3=B32-63\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\smallskip
{\footnotesize \textbf{RD\_ACCUM} is the width/rate matcher (knows gear); RD\_SRAM stays gear-agnostic
--- one wide 512b write per packet, not per beat.}

\section{Points to get right (for your diagram)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Read is \textbf{PHY-timed}: capture on \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_valid}; don't assume a fixed en$\to$data
      offset the MC controls (that was the write side).
\item \texttt{dfi\_rddata\_en} = window the MC opens (cmd+tphy\_rdlat, hold BL/2); \texttt{rddata\_valid}
      = PHY says data is here. Two different signals, two owners.
\item \textbf{One wide 512b write} to RD\_SRAM per packet (RD\_ACCUM gathers first); not per-beat.
\item \texttt{rd\_done} = \textbf{last beat committed to RD\_SRAM}, never before --- completion can't fire
      until the final beat is in.
\item \texttt{dbuf\_addr} + \texttt{rob\_index} ride the \textbf{RL delay line} from RD issue; no CAM.
\item Multiple reads in flight: RL line depth $\approx$ RL/spacing (like the write WL line) --- a
      \textbf{delay line}, not one counter (same reasoning as the write path).
\item \texttt{tphy\_rdlat} \textbf{same across ranks}; gear only rescales the DFI-cycle unit + capture width.
\end{itemize}

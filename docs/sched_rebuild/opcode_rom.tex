\begin{center}
{\LARGE\textbf{DFI OPCODE\_ROM --- Command Truth Table (JESD79-5 Table 241)}}\\[2pt]
{\small One channel, DDR5. \textbf{cmd\_type} $\rightarrow$ the fixed CA H/L pattern per UI.
\colorbox{opc}{green} = opcode bit the ROM drives fixed; \colorbox{fld}{yellow} = field position FIELD\_MUX
fills (row/col/BG/BA/CID/AP/BL/MRA/OP). H=1, L=0, V=valid (H or L), X=don't care.
\textbf{CA1} = cycle count (L$\Rightarrow$2-UI, H$\Rightarrow$1-UI); \textbf{CA0}=L only for ACT.}
\end{center}

\section{How to read this chart}
{\small
\textbf{Each row = one command.} A 2-cycle command uses two rows (UI 0 = first clock beat,
UI 1 = second); a 1-cycle command is one row. \textbf{Columns CA0\dots CA13 = the 14 physical wires
of the DDR5 command bus} (one shared bus; there are no separate row/col/bank pins). \textbf{Each cell
= what that wire must be} for this command on this beat.

\smallskip
\textbf{Cell values:} \texttt{H}=drive high (1), \texttt{L}=drive low (0) --- these are the fixed
\emph{command fingerprint} the DRAM decodes. \texttt{V}=valid (either level, ignored by decode).
\texttt{X}=don't care. A \textbf{name} = that wire carries an address/attribute bit:
\texttt{R0..R17}=row, \texttt{C2..C10}=column, \texttt{BA0/BA1}=bank, \texttt{BG0..BG2}=bank group,
\texttt{CID}=chip ID, \texttt{AP}=auto-precharge, \texttt{BL}=burst length, \texttt{MRA/OP}=mode-reg
addr/data, \texttt{CW}=control word, \texttt{WRp}=write-partial.

\smallskip
\textbf{Colors:} \colorbox{opc}{green} = a fixed H/L the OPCODE\_ROM hardwires (the fingerprint).
\colorbox{fld}{yellow} = a field the FIELD\_MUX fills from the request (bank, row, column, \dots).

\smallskip
\textbf{Worked example --- Read (RD), 2 cycles on the same 14 wires:}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{l C C C C C C C C C C C C C C}
\toprule
\rowcolor{hb}UI & CA0 & CA1 & CA2 & CA3 & CA4 & CA5 & CA6 & CA7 & CA8 & CA9 & CA10 & CA11 & CA12 & CA13\\ \midrule
0 & \op{H} & \op{L} & \op{H} & \op{H} & \op{H} & \fd{BL} & \fd{BA0} & \fd{BA1} & \fd{BG0} & \fd{BG1} & \fd{BG2} & \fd{CID} & \fd{CID} & \fd{CID}\\
1 & \fd{C2} & \fd{C3} & \fd{C4} & \fd{C5} & \fd{C6} & \fd{C7} & \fd{C8} & \fd{C9} & \fd{C10} & V & \fd{AP} & V & V & \fd{CID}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Cycle 1: green \texttt{CA0..CA4 = H\,L\,H\,H\,H} is the pattern that \emph{means} ``read''; at the
same time bank on CA6/7, bank group on CA8--10, burst length on CA5. Cycle 2: put the \textbf{column}
address on the wires, auto-precharge on CA10. Two beats, same wires reused.

\smallskip
\textbf{How the DRAM decodes any row (the tree):}
(1) \texttt{CA1} first --- \texttt{L}=2-cycle, \texttt{H}=1-cycle.
(2) \texttt{CA0} --- \texttt{L}=ACT, else keep going.
(3) \texttt{CA2/CA3/CA4} pick the command (e.g.\ within 2-cycle: CA3=L$\Rightarrow$mode-reg op,
CA3=H$\Rightarrow$CAS, then CA4 = WR(L)/RD(H)).
(4) latch the yellow address fields, execute. Reading a row = ``green wires say \emph{which}
command; yellow wires carry the \emph{address} for it, spread over one or two beats.''
}

{\footnotesize REF/RFM family: CA0--CA4 all \texttt{1\,1\,0\,0\,1}; variant is \textbf{CA9}
(REF=H / RFM=L) and \textbf{CA10} (sb=H / ab=L), plus CA6/CA7=BA for the sb forms. PRE family:

\vspace{3pt}
{\footnotesize \textbf{Decode summary:} CA1 = 1-vs-2 cycle; CA0=L $\Rightarrow$ ACT; within CA0=H,CA1=L
(2-UI) CA2=H then CA3 splits MR-ops (L) from CAS (H) and CA4 splits WR (L) / RD (H); within
CA0=H,CA1=H (1-UI) CA2/CA3/CA4 pick the VrefCA/REF/PRE/SRE/MPC/NOP group and CA9/CA10 pick the
REF/RFM $\times$ sb/ab and PRE sb/ab variant. \textbf{ap} = UI1 CA10 for RD/WR only.}

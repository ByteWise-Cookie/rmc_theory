\begin{center}
{\LARGE\textbf{Per-command DFI drive list}}\\[2pt]
{\small DDR5, one channel. For each command: which DFI signals it drives + one-line info.
Colors: \ca{CA/command}, \dq{data-path}, \pw{power/CKE}. Every issued phase also drives
\ca{dfi\_cs\_pN} (assert target rank one-cold, else DES) and \ca{dfi\_address\_pN} (CA beat);
idle phases = DES. UI = command length in unit intervals.}
\end{center}

\section*{}
\begin{drive}
ACT & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}(rank, UI0), \ca{dfi\_address}(ACT op + bg + bank + row; row split UI0/UI1)
   & Open row. No data path. Stamps tRC/tRCD/tRAS.\\
RD & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(RD op + bg + bank + col + ap); then \dq{dfi\_rddata\_en}
   (@tphy\_rdlat); @RL: \dq{dfi\_rddata\_wN}, \dq{dfi\_rddata\_valid} (in)
   & Column read; data returns on DQ (RL=CL). Arms RD\_CAP (sram=dbuf).\\
WR & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(WR op + bg + bank + col + ap); \pw{dfi\_odt}(RTT\_WR win);
   then \dq{dfi\_wrdata\_en}, \dq{dfi\_wrdata}, \dq{dfi\_wrdata\_mask}, \dq{dfi\_wrdata\_cs} (@tphy\_wrlat)
   & Column write; drives data on DQ (CWL). Arms WL\_LAUNCH (sram=wd\_slot).\\
PRE & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(PRE op + bg + bank)
   & Close one row. Stamps tRP.\\
PREA & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(PREA op)
   & Precharge all banks in rank.\\
PREsb & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(PREsb op + bank k)
   & Precharge bank k across all BGs (refresh drain).\\
REF & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(REFab op)
   & All-bank refresh; gate\_rfc all banks (tRFC1).\\
REFsb & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(REFsb op + bank k)
   & Same-bank refresh (8 banks); gate\_rfc (tRFCsb).\\
RFM & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(RFM op)
   & Refresh management; RAA-=RAAIMT.\\
MRW & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(MRW op + MRA + data)
   & Write mode register; gate\_mr (tMRD), live +tMOD. No DQ.\\
MRR & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(MRR op + MRA); \dq{dfi\_rddata\_en}; @RL: \dq{dfi\_rddata}(in)
   & Read mode register; MR data on DQ -> RD\_CAP (sideband, rob tag).\\
MPC/ZQ & 2 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(MPC op); \dq{PHY DQ cal}
   & Multi-purpose (ZQ cal etc); gate\_zq (tZQCAL), latch tZQLAT.\\
SRE & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(SRE op) + \pw{dfi\_cke}$\downarrow$
   & Self-refresh entry (CA cmd + CKE low). DRAM self-refreshes.\\
SRX & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}, \ca{dfi\_address}(SRX op) + \pw{dfi\_cke}$\uparrow$
   & Self-refresh exit (CKE high, then re-init timing).\\
PDE & -- & \pw{dfi\_cke}$\downarrow$ (DES on CA)
   & Power-down entry. NOT a CA command - pure CKE.\\
PDX & -- & \pw{dfi\_cke}$\uparrow$
   & Power-down exit. Pure CKE.\\
DES & 1 & \ca{dfi\_cs}=high (deselect), \ca{dfi\_address}=don't-care
   & No-op; fills idle phase slots.\\
\end{drive}

\section*{Always-driven / background}
\begin{tabular}{>{\ttfamily\footnotesize}l >{\footnotesize\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{13cm}}
\toprule
dfi\_cs\_pN & every phase: target rank one-cold (active-low) on a command's UI0, else all-high (DES).\\
dfi\_address\_pN & every phase: the CA beat for whatever command occupies that slot (or don't-care on DES).\\
dfi\_reset\_n & held by INIT\_FSM during reset; high in normal operation.\\
dfi\_cke\_pN & steady high in normal op; driven low only by SRE/PDE (POWER\_MGMT).\\
dfi\_odt\_pN & driven around writes (RTT\_WR window, WL-offset); parked/nom otherwise.\\
dfi\_parity\_in & CA parity, tparin\_lat after each command (if parity enabled).\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{5pt}
{\footnotesize \textbf{Note.} \ca{CA signals} (dfi\_cs / dfi\_address) are driven by DFI\_CMD\_ENCODE
for every command (one CA bus). \dq{Data-path signals} are driven later by WL\_LAUNCH / RD\_CAP at
the PHY latency (tphy\_wrlat / tphy\_rdlat) - only for RD/WR/MRR. \pw{CKE} is POWER\_MGMT.
The \texttt{2ui} flag (separate sched$\rightarrow$DFI wire) tells the driver 1-UI vs 2-UI.}
